Help & Support

Getting Started

  1. Grant Camera Permission: Click the extension icon and allow camera access when prompted.
  2. Start Tracking: Click "Start Tracking" in the popup to begin eye tracking.
  3. Calibrate: Run calibration for better accuracy (recommended).
  4. Navigate: Look around the screen to move the gaze cursor.
  5. Click: Double blink to click on elements.

Troubleshooting

Camera permission denied?

Check your browser's camera settings and ensure EyeTrack has permission to access your camera.

Tracking not accurate?

Try running calibration and ensure good lighting conditions. Position yourself 50-70cm from the screen.

Performance issues?

Lower the update rate in tracking settings or close other camera-using applications.
